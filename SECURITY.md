# Security policy

This project ships with fake demo data only. Do not point it at real patient data without a privacy and
legal review (Pakistan has no enacted comprehensive data-protection law at the time of writing, and a draft
bill is pending; take local legal advice).

Report a vulnerability privately to the repository owner (GitHub "Report a vulnerability"), not in a public
issue. Please include steps to reproduce. Secrets belong in environment variables, never in the repository.
